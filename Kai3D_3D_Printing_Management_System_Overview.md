# Kai3D - 3D Printing Management System (3DPMS)

## Vision

Kai3D is more than an online shop. It is a complete **3D Printing Management System (3DPMS)** that combines e-commerce, production management, inventory, reporting, customer management, and future AI-powered product customization into one scalable platform.
